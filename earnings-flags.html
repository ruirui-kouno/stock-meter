How a flag works

A fixed rule, applied the same way every day. No analyst discretion, no retroactive edits.

  • Trigger: an earnings-forecast cut of 50% or more, or a swing from profit to net loss, in a company disclosure.
  • Same-day flag: the stock is flagged the day the disclosure is published, with the before/after numbers and the stated reason.
  • Scores suspended: while flagged, buy-timing and 10x scores are withheld — a collapsing stock must never look like a bargain.
  • Clear rule: the flag lifts only on a new disclosure (recovery, next results, or a new large order). No silent expiry.
  • English only: Japanese disclosures, translated to the numbers that matter. No Japanese required.

What this is not

No buy or sell timing. No price targets. No investment advice. This is a data feed: a flag, the numbers behind it, and the source. Nothing more.
